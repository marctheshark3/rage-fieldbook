# Evidence note

- **Question and expected behavior:**
- **Basis for the expectation:** Requirement, contract, or separately established baseline.
- **Source identity:** Public URL, exact revision, and relevant file or artifact.
- **Evidence kind:** Source inspection, fictional illustration, executed fixture, simulation, physical observation, or reported result.
- **Conditions:** Inputs, environment, relevant state, and known substitutions.
- **Procedure:** Command or action sequence; what was captured.
- **Observation:** Include failures and exclusions as well as successes.
- **Comparison:** Baseline, changed condition, and any unmatched conditions.
- **Interpretation:** Which claim the observation supports.
- **Limits:** What it leaves unmeasured or cannot distinguish.
- **Decision:** Adopt, reject, investigate further, or leave unresolved.
- **Next check:** What observation would change the decision?

Use “reported” when relying on another author's result. Use “reproduced” only when recording the execution and conditions that reproduced it.
